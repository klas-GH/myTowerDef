Quick Guide - myTowerDef v2.14.1

1. Run it
Save files in same folder. Open index.html in browser.
If local files blocked, use VS Code Live Server.

2. How to play
- Tap a green spot, then choose Archer (50), Cannon (60), or Magic (80) from the popup. Start with 80 coins + 3% per level above 1, enough for any tower. Magic deals full damage to its target and half damage to enemies within 38px.
- Tap an existing tower to select and upgrade it, or Sell it for a full refund.
- Tap Start Wave to begin.
- Targeting: tap a tower to select it, then change its target priority (First, Strongest, Fastest, Nearest) on the upgrade panel. Free to change; affects which enemy it attacks next.
- The wave panel shows the next wave's enemy types and counts before you start it.
- Enemy types: Standard (red), Runner (fast, low HP), Armored (slow, high HP).
- Defend base with 6 starting lives. Survive all waves to win (+coins per wave).
- Clear all 3 waves to move to the next level. There are 12 maps, and enemy strength increases with each level.
- A gentle synthesized soundtrack and tower firing sounds begin after your first tap. Toggle background music and sound effects separately in Settings.
- Enemies begin with 65% more HP, Armored enemies have extra base HP, and speed increases another 5% each wave.
- Finish all 12 levels to open a new round. Enemy HP and speed increase by 1% each round, with a popup announcing the change. After 50 rounds, a special victory popup celebrates your run and announces the restart at Round #1.
- Restart to play again.

3. Challenges (v2.3.0)
- Tap the 🏆 button to open the challenge panel. Each challenge is a one-attempt test on a specific level with its own starting coins, tower restrictions, and objective.
- Completing a challenge awards Bronze, Silver, or Gold medals based on lives remaining. Your best medal and attempt count persist across reloads.
- Challenge attempts never affect campaign progress, achievements, or statistics.

4. Notes
- Mobile-first responsive layout with touch input.
- Offline-capable via service worker.
- Towers can be upgraded up to level 3.
- Pixel-art biome backgrounds are generated locally; no external art or audio downloads are needed.
